Anne has worked as a physiotherapist since 2015 and qualified as an osteopath in 2021. She has a particular focus on babies and children, for example with reflux, colic or a flat head, and on adults with chronic pain. She always starts with a thorough conversation and examination, so the treatment addresses what lies behind your symptoms.
