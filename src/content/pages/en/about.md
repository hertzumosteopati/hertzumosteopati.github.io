Anne treats babies, children and adults with her hands and always looks at the whole body, not only the place that hurts. She puts weight on a thorough conversation and examination, so the treatment addresses the cause of your symptoms and not only the symptoms themselves.

## Background

Anne has worked in clinics since 2014, first as a physiotherapist with manual treatment and examination, individual and group training, and rehabilitation after surgery at a private hospital.

In 2018 she started working with Michael Cohrt at Manuel Medicinsk Klinik in Nykøbing Sjælland. She followed him for several months and learned a great deal about treating babies and children. In 2021 she qualified as an osteopath, and today she also has her own clinic in Brøndby.

## Education

- Osteopath (MSc, M.D.O.), The International Academy of Osteopathy, 2021
- Physiotherapist (BSc), UCS in Roskilde, 2015
- Further training in craniosacral therapy, OMFT and treatment of infants and children
- Authorised as osteopath and as physiotherapist, member of Danske Osteopater

## Focus

Anne has a particular focus on babies and children, for example with reflux, colic or a flat head, on children and young people, and on adults with chronic pain.

In Brøndby Anne can treat both as osteopath and as physiotherapist. Treatment is available in Danish and English.
